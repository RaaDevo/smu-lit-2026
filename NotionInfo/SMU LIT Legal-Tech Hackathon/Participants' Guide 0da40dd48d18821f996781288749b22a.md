## Judging Criteria

1. **Relevance to Problem Statement (30%)**
Does the solution address the problem statement the team selected, and does it solve a real, well-understood pain point in that area of legal practice?
2. **Technical Feasibility (25%)**
Does the solution work, and is it built in a way that could realistically be developed further? Includes code quality, use of AI tools, and whether the team can explain their own build.
3. **Innovation (25%)**
Does the solution offer a genuinely new approach, or a meaningfully better way of solving an existing problem, within the legal-tech space?
4. **Presentation Quality (20%)**
Was the pitch clear, well-structured, and convincing within the 4-minute time limit, and did the team handle questions well?
